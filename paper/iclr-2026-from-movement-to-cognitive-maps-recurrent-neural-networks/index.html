From movement to cognitive maps: recurrent neural networks reveal how locomotor development shapes hippocampal spatial coding

ICLROral2026

Authors
Marco P Abrate, Laurenz Muessig, Joshua P Bassett, Hui Min Tan, Francesca Cacucci, Thomas Joseph Wills, Caswell Barry
Affiliation
University College London, University of London
Venue
ICLR 2026
Track
Oral

TL;DR

The hippocampus contains neurons whose firing correlates with an animal's location and orientation in space. Collectively, these neurons are held to support a cognitive map of the environment, enabling the recall of and navigation to specific locations.
