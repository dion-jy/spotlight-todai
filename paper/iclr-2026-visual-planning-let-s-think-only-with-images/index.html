Visual Planning: Let's Think Only with Images

ICLROral2026

Authors

Affiliation
Mistral AI

TL;DR

Recent advancements in Large Language Models (LLMs) and their multimodal extensions (MLLMs) have substantially enhanced machine reasoning across diverse tasks. However, these models predominantly rely on pure text as the medium for both expressing and structuring reasoning, even when visual information is present.
