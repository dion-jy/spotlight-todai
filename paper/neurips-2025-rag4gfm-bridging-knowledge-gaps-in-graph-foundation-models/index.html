RAG4GFM: Bridging Knowledge Gaps in Graph Foundation Models through Graph Retrieval Augmented Generation

NeurIPSOral2025

Authors
Xingliang Wang, Zemin Liu, Junxiao Han, Shuiguang Deng
Affiliation
Zhejiang University
Venue
NeurIPS 2025
Track
Oral

TL;DR

Graph Foundation Models (GFMs) have demonstrated remarkable potential across graph learning tasks but face significant challenges in knowledge updating and reasoning faithfulness. To address these issues, we introduce the Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) paradigm for GFMs, which leverages graph knowledge retrieval.
