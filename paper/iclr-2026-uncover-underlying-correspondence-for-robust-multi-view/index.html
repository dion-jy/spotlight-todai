Uncover Underlying Correspondence for Robust Multi-view Clustering

ICLROral2026

Authors
Haochen Zhou, Guofeng Ding, Mouxing Yang, Peng Hu, Yijie Lin, Xi Peng
Affiliation
Sichuan University
Venue
ICLR 2026
Track
Oral

TL;DR

Multi-view clustering (MVC) aims to group unlabeled data into semantically meaningful clusters by leveraging cross-view consistency. However, real-world datasets collected from the web often suffer from noisy correspondence (NC), which breaks the consistency prior and results in unreliable alignments.
