ElasticMM: Efficient Multimodal LLMs Serving with Elastic Multimodal Parallelism

TL;DR

Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) extend LLMs to handle images, videos, and audio by incorporating feature extractors and projection modules. However, these additional components—combined with complex inference pipelines and heterogeneous workloads—introduce significant inference overhead.
