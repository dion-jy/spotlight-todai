EmotionThinker: Prosody-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Explainable Speech Emotion Reasoning

TL;DR

Emotional information in speech plays a unique role in multimodal perception. However, current Speech Large Language Models (SpeechLLMs), similar to conventional speech emotion recognition (SER) systems, still treat emotion understanding as a simple classification problem.
