Extending Sequence Length is Not All You Need: Effective Integration of Multimodal Signals for Gene Expression Prediction

ICLROral2026

Authors
Zhao Yang, Yi Duan, Jiwei Zhu, Ying Ba, Chuan Cao, Bing Su
Affiliation
Renmin University of China
Venue
ICLR 2026
Track

TL;DR

Gene expression prediction, which predicts mRNA expression levels from DNA sequences, presents significant challenges. Previous works often focus on extending input sequence length to locate distal enhancers, which may influence target genes from hundreds of kilobases away.
