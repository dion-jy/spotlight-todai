Universal Inverse Distillation for Matching Models with Real-Data Supervision (No GANs)

ICLROral2026

Authors
Nikita Maksimovich Kornilov, David Li, Tikhon Mavrin, Aleksei Leonov, Nikita Gushchin, Evgeny Burnaev, Iaroslav Sergeevich Koshelev, Alexander Korotin
Affiliation
Applied AI Institute
Venue
ICLR 2026
Track
Oral

TL;DR

While achieving exceptional generative quality, modern diffusion, flow, and other matching models suffer from slow inference, as they require many steps of iterative generation. Recent distillation methods address this problem by training efficient one-step generators under the guidance of a pre-trained teacher model.
