Tighter CMI-Based Generalization Bounds via Stochastic Projection and Quantization

NeurIPSOral2025

Authors
Milad Sefidgaran, Kimia Nadjahi, Abdellatif Zaidi
Affiliation
Huawei Paris Research Center
Venue
NeurIPS 2025
Track
Oral

TL;DR

In this paper, we leverage stochastic projection and lossy compression to establish new conditional mutual information (CMI) bounds on the generalization error of statistical learning algorithms. It is shown that these bounds are generally tighter than the existing ones.
