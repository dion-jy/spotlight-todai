Mean Flows for One-step Generative Modeling

NeurIPSOral2025

Authors
Zhengyang Geng, Mingyang Deng, Xingjian Bai, J Zico Kolter, Kaiming He
Affiliation
Carnegie Mellon University
Venue
NeurIPS 2025
Track
Oral

TL;DR

We propose a principled and effective framework for one-step generative modeling. We introduce the notion of average velocity to characterize flow fields, in contrast to instantaneous velocity modeled by Flow Matching methods.
