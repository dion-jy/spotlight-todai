WSM: Decay-Free Learning Rate Schedule via Checkpoint Merging for LLM Pre-training

TL;DR

Recent advances in learning rate~(LR) scheduling have demonstrated the effectiveness of decay-free approaches that eliminate the traditional decay phase while maintaining competitive performance. Model merging techniques have emerged as particularly promising solutions in this domain.
