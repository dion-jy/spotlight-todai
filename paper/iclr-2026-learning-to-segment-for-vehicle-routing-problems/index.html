Learning to Segment for Vehicle Routing Problems

ICLROral2026

Authors
Wenbin Ouyang, Sirui Li, Yining Ma, Cathy Wu
Affiliation
Massachusetts Institute of Technology
Venue
ICLR 2026
Track
Oral

TL;DR

Iterative heuristics are widely recognized as state-of-the-art for Vehicle Routing Problems (VRPs). In this work, we exploit a critical observation: a large portion of the solution remains stable, i.e., unchanged across search iterations, causing redundant computations, especially for large-scale VRPs with long subtours.
