Pinet: Optimizing hard-constrained neural networks with orthogonal projection layers

ICLROral2026

Authors
Panagiotis D. Grontas, Antonio Terpin, Efe C. Balta, Raffaello D'Andrea, John Lygeros
Affiliation
International Business Machines
Venue
ICLR 2026
Track
Oral

TL;DR

We introduce an output layer for neural networks that ensures satisfaction of convex constraints. Our approach, $\Pi$net, leverages operator splitting for rapid and reliable projections in the forward pass, and the implicit function theorem for backpropagation.
